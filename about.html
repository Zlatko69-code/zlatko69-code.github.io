About

Zlatko69 is the creator brand of Zlatko Chulev - Zekir. On itch.io he publishes premium digital assets for game developers, designers and content creators.

The catalogue covers:

  • Game assets & VFX: smooth ready-to-use effects, UI packs, music and SFX
  • Tools: Godot 4 addons and offline browser tools (Weather & Time-of-Day Director, Retro 32-bit Texture Studio, Voltblip, Godot Map Forge, Pixmelt, EffectDeck, TagPress)
  • AI prompt packs: video, vlog and UGC ad workflows (Persona to Post, Frame to Feed, Tailor-Made and more)
  • Wallpapers & printables: phone and PC wallpapers, star posters, tarot and design bundles
  • Games: small games and solo letter-writing titles
  • Clean, ready-to-use files with new drops arriving regularly
  • Some products use AI tools, always disclosed on each product page

Every product is sold and delivered through itch.io. This site is a catalogue and contact page for the same store.
